| Stratum | χ²_F | p-value | Kendall's W | Interpretation |

### Thinking Mode Contrasts (Bonus Analysis)

| Comparison | p-value | Significant? |

**Headline finding:** Extended "thinking mode" does NOT significantly reduce security vulnerabilities in LLM-generated IaC.
